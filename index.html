📖 Pro Level Candlestick & Chart Pattern Visual Guide

Candlestick patterns ko diagrams aur charts ke sath live samjhein.

Bullish Reversal 1. Bullish Hammer

Downtrend ke bottom par banta hai. Chhoti body aur long lower wick hoti hai, jo strong buyers entry ka signal deti hai.

Bearish Reversal 2. Shooting Star

Uptrend ke top par banta hai. Iski upper wick badi hoti hai jo batata hai ki sellers market ko niche khinch rahe hain.

Bullish Reversal 3. Bullish Engulfing

Ek chhoti red candle ke baad badi green candle aati hai jo red candle ko poori tarah engulf kar leti hai. Strong BUY setup!

Chart Pattern 4. Double Bottom (W Pattern)

Market 2 baar ek hi support area ko touch karke W shape banata hai. Neckline breakout hote hi CALL option buy karein.
